# Reply Playbook

Principles per moment for every AI-drafted reply in the CRMS (Leads, Follow
Ups, Relationships, drips, Telegram). Runtime-read by `lib/reply/` once
Phase 1 of `BRIEF_REPLY_PLANNER_2026-09-24.md` ships.

Rules of this file:
- **Principles, never example sentences.** Example phrasings ship verbatim.
- Ryan does not write entries. Entries come from his why-sentences: one is a
  question, three that rhyme become a proposed principle he approves.
- Each moment has a `Pending whys` list. The weekly review turns them into
  principles or drops them.
- Change the `version` when a principle changes; it is stamped on every draft.

## Leads

### soft_no
They declined, softly, without opting out. "Not at this time", "not interested
right now", "we're holding onto it".

Principles:
1. Acknowledge cleanly. No "but", no re-pitch, no reframing their answer.
2. Thank them for replying. They did not have to.
3. One door, on their side. Reaching out later is their move to make.
4. Honest notice that we'll stay in touch occasionally. This is the consent for
   the drip that follows; without it the first nurture touch feels ignored.
5. Name the trigger moment for their situation in one clause (tenant leaving,
   a big repair, a change of plans, an estate) so they associate Ryan with
   that moment.
6. No valuation, no offer, no number. A declined ask is not an open ask.
7. Next action defaults to long-term nurture. A nurture touch after a soft no
   may not re-pitch or ask whether they are ready to sell.

Observations from the 60 edited sends, 2026-09-24 (not principles yet; bring
to Ryan when the eval is graded):
- Ryan's edits add one specific shared memory or a piece of real news
  (where they met, a deal that closed, the baby, a meetup), and strip the
  generic "looking for my next project" line or shorten it.

  removed every time. Specifics must come from the thread or notes, never

## Drips

A drip touch inherits the moment of the last inbound message. The principles
for that moment apply to every touch that follows it, in addition to the
drip's own cadence rules.
