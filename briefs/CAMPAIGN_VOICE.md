# Campaign voice rules (read by the compose prompt)

> The compose step reads the RULES block below verbatim into the prompt for
> every T1 variant body. Edit this file to change how drafts sound. Keep it
> short: every line costs tokens on every draft. Rules are written ONLY when
> Ryan confirms a pattern (edits are questions, not rules). Rulebook version
> is stamped on each send as `prompt_version`.

<!-- RULES START -->
1. Sound like a busy investor writing a short note to an agent. Plain words, short sentences. Warm-up openers are fine.
2. Keep every fact in the template (as-is, quick close, South Bay focus, under $4M). A reassurance is fine; do not pile several on.
3. Never reference a past property, listing, or transaction. The address in the data is an MLS tie, not history. A region or brokerage from CONTEXT is fine.
4. Do not invent history: no "we spoke before", "been meaning to reach out", or similar unless the template says it.
5. The ask is "send it my way" (reply or text). A call is only offered to discuss a specific property. Never offer to meet, get coffee, or visit.
6. Do not contrast the ask against email ("instead of going back and forth", "rather talk than trade emails"). Just make the ask.
7. Dashes, bullets, and emojis are allowed when they read naturally. No links. No exclamation-heavy tone.
<!-- RULES END -->
