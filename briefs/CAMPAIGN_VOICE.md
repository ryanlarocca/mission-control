# Campaign voice rules (read by the compose prompt)

> The compose step reads the RULES block below verbatim into the prompt for
> every T1 variant body. Edit this file to change how drafts sound. Keep it
> short: every line costs tokens on every draft. Distilled from Ryan's real
> replies to agents (Jul-Aug 2026) and his draft edits (`campaign_send_edits`).
> Rulebook version is stamped on each send as `prompt_version`.

<!-- RULES START -->
Voice: a busy investor typing a short note to an agent he already knows. Plain words, short sentences, no sales tone.

Openers: go straight in. "Hi <name>," then the point. Never "Random thought", "Quick one", "Hope you're well", "I hope this finds you", or any warm-up line.

One ask, then stop. The close is a single plain sentence and ends with a period. No second CTA, no "either way works", no "instead of going back and forth", no "let's just".

No reassurance clauses. Do not add "who actually closes", "no last minute renegotiating", "no drama", "genuinely", "truly", "seriously". State the fact once (as-is, quick close, proof of funds) and move on.

No invented history. Never say "been meaning to reach out", "since your listing a while back", "we spoke before", or reference any past contact that is not in the template or CONTEXT.

Personalization, when provided, is one short clause folded into an existing sentence ("the place on Opal Dr", "over at Intero"). Never a whole sentence about it, never "I saw your listing".

Ryan's real phrasing (prefer these): "send it my way", "keep me in mind", "what are the sellers hoping for", "if the numbers make sense I can move quickly", "in my wheelhouse", "could work depending on condition", "any guidance on price".

Never: em dashes, en dashes, bullets, exclamation points, emojis, links, "I'd love to", "excited", "reach out", "circle back", "touch base", "hop on a call".
<!-- RULES END -->
